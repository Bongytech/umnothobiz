# UMNOTHO Community Guidelines

## Our Values
UMNOTHO is a portfolio project built for learning and collaboration. We value:
- **Respectful collaboration** and constructive feedback
- **Inclusive participation** from diverse backgrounds
- **Educational focus** on sharing knowledge and skills
- **Professional conduct** in all interactions

## Expected Behavior
- Communicate with kindness and respect
- Provide constructive, actionable feedback
- Be patient with learners and new contributors
- Respect different perspectives and approaches
- Help create a welcoming environment for all

## Unacceptable Behavior
- Personal attacks, insults, or derogatory comments
- Harassment in any form (sexual, racial, etc.)
- Publishing private information without consent
- Deliberate intimidation or bullying
- Any form of discrimination

## Enforcement
Project maintainers are responsible for upholding these guidelines. Violations may result in:
- Private correction and education
- Temporary or permanent restriction from participation
- Removal of inappropriate content or contributions

All enforcement decisions will be made fairly and consistently.

## Reporting Issues
If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior:
1. Contact the project maintainer at bongytech@gmail.com
2. Include relevant details (links, screenshots, etc.)
3. All reports will be reviewed and addressed promptly

## Scope
These guidelines apply to all UMNOTHO spaces including:
- GitHub repositories (issues, PRs, discussions)
- Project documentation and communication channels
- Any events or interactions related to the project

## Acknowledgement
By participating in this project, you agree to follow these guidelines and help create a positive community experience for everyone.

---

*These guidelines are designed to foster a collaborative learning environment for this educational portfolio project.*
